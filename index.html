How it works

From lost to back in your hands.

Post what you lost or found. Reunyt does the matching and keeps both of you safe until it's handed over.

  1. 01

    Post it

    Say what you lost or found, when, and where. Use your location, drop a pin or search for a place. Speak instead of typing if you like.

  2. 02

    Get matched

    Reunyt shows possible matches nearby. For an ID card, passport, driving licence or bank card, the number you type finds the exact one.

  3. 03

    Prove it's yours

    Answer the finder's questions about the item. The finder decides, and only then can the two of you message in the app.

  4. 04

    Hand over safely

    Meet at a police station or another busy public place. The owner shows a handover code, and the finder scans or types it to finish.

Privacy first

Your exact spot stays private.

Others only ever see an area about 500 m across, never the place you were standing. You talk inside the app, so nobody swaps phone numbers or emails with a stranger.

  • Document and card numbers are never shown to anyone
  • Messages open only after the finder accepts a claim
  • Report or block anyone. We review reports within 24 hours
  • No ads, no tracking, and you can delete your account at any time

For finders

Found something? Doing the right thing takes a minute.

Post it, and Reunyt shows you the easiest way to get it back to its owner.

Phones, wallets, keys and bags

Add a question or two only the owner could answer. You read the answers and decide who gets it back. Never ask for a PIN: for a phone, ask the owner to unlock it in front of you.

ID documents and bank cards

Hand them in at a police station or Home Affairs office, with the reference number on a note. The owner is told where, and shows proof of identity at the counter.

Stay in the loop

Hear about it without checking.

Notifications only say what happened. They never include item details, names or places.

Possible matches

When something that could be yours is posted nearby, or your ID is handed in.

My area

Watch one area, like your neighbourhood, within 1, 2 or 5 km. At most 3 alerts a day.

Notify me

Follow someone else's post and hear when it's reunited. The owner only sees how many people are looking out.
