An example

How Reunyt would work for the SPCA

Today, an owner whose dog runs away phones round every shelter and vet, and scrolls Facebook groups. With Reunyt, the shelter posts once and the right owners are told.

  1. A dog is brought in

    Staff post it in a minute: dog, colour and markings, a photo, and the place it was found. The post is marked with the branch's verified badge.

  2. The right owners hear about it

    Anyone who posted a lost dog near where it was found is told "Something like your dog was posted nearby". Dogs and cats are matched separately, within 10 km by default. People watching that area hear too.

  3. The post says where it is now

    "Now at" the branch, with its address and opening hours. Nobody needs to phone round, and the exact place it was found stays private.

  4. The owner collects at the counter

    They show their handover code at the counter, and the post closes. Everyone following it hears it's back home.

Pet posts with photos are in the app now. Organisation accounts come next. This describes how Reunyt is being designed for animal shelters such as the SPCA. It isn't a partnership: Reunyt is independent.

Safety

The same rules protect your staff, finders and owners.

  • Where something was found is shown only as an area about 500 m across.
  • A handover code confirms the right person got it back.
  • Every post and conversation can be reported, and we review reports within 24 hours.
  • Reunyt is independent. It isn't run by the police or any government office.
