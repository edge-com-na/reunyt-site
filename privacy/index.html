Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

The short version. The full policy below is what counts.

  • We collect what Reunyt needs to work: your email, a display name, your reports, claims and messages, and where things were lost or found.
  • Other people only ever see an approximate area about 500 m across, never the exact spot.
  • Document and card numbers are never shown to anyone. They are only used to find an exact match.
  • Voice input is turned into text on your phone. The audio never leaves it.
  • Pet photos are shrunk on your phone and stripped of hidden details, including where they were taken, before they're uploaded.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Reunyt is operated by Edge Technology Solutions (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in Namibia ("we", "us" or "our"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. It applies to the Reunyt app and the website at reunyt.com, and should be read with our Terms of Use.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form, so nobody can read it), your display name, the profile icon you choose, and when you accepted our Terms.
  • Reports: whether you lost or found something, its category, title, description and date, and where it happened. Each report also gets a reference number that only you see.
  • Location of a report: the exact point you choose (your current location, a pin on the map or a place you search for), how you chose it and, for a GPS reading, its accuracy. We publish only the approximate area. The exact point is kept in a locked part of our database that no user can read.
  • Document and card details: for ID documents, the document type, issuing country and number. For bank cards, only the last 4 digits and the surname on the card, never the full card number. These are kept in a locked part of our database and are only used to find an exact match between a lost and a found report.
  • Claims and messages: your answers to a finder's questions, any note you add, messages between a finder and an owner after a claim is accepted, the handover point you choose, and whether a handover code was used.
  • Hand-ins and thanks: where and when a found item was handed in, whether the owner collected it or found it wasn't there, and any thank-you note.
  • Safety: reports you make about other users or content (the reason, any note, and a copy of what you reported), people you block, and our decisions on reports.
  • Settings: your alert radius for nearby matches. Other defaults, such as where Browse starts, stay on your phone.
  • Pet photos: one photo of the animal on a pet post. A found pet's photo is taken in the app. A lost pet's photo can be taken in the app or chosen from your photos, and Reunyt receives only the photo you choose. Before it leaves your phone, the photo is shrunk and every hidden detail is removed, including where and when it was taken and the camera used.
  • Where a found pet's photo was taken: when you post a found pet, we record where your phone was when you took its photo, if location is allowed for Reunyt. It's kept private, never shown to anyone, and used only to check reports of misuse, such as fake finds.
  • My area: if you choose to watch an area, we keep only an area about 500 m across, never the exact spot, and the distance you choose, to send you at most 3 alerts a day.
  • Notify me: the posts you choose to follow. This is private: a post's owner sees only how many people are looking out for it, never who.
  • Messages to us: anything you send us, for example by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your phone's push notification token, so we can send you alerts.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps server logs, such as IP addresses and the times of requests, for security and troubleshooting.

Anonymous counts

If you leave Share anonymous counts on (Settings › Privacy), the app sends daily totals that help us improve matching, such as how often a suggested colour was kept or where in a list of matches the right one was. They never include who you are, your posts, photos or location, and they can't be traced back to you. You can turn this off at any time, and the app then counts and sends nothing.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and there are no third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app. We don't access your contacts, and we never see your photo library as a whole: when you choose a photo for a lost pet, Reunyt receives only that one photo.

4. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • create and run your account, and keep you signed in;
  • show reports and find possible and exact matches between lost and found items;
  • let finders and owners claim, message and arrange handovers, and show where handed-in items are;
  • send you notifications you have allowed, including alerts for an area you watch and posts you follow;
  • keep Reunyt safe: review reports of abuse, block, hide or remove content and accounts, filter offensive words, limit misuse such as fake or repeated posts, and prevent fraud;
  • answer your questions and support requests;
  • fix problems and improve Reunyt;
  • meet our legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

We only use your information for these purposes. We don't use it for advertising or sell it to anyone.

6. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers who run parts of Reunyt for us and may only use your information to do so:
    • Supabase, for our database, photo storage, sign-in and server functions;
    • Apple, to deliver push notifications and for map place search (searches you type are sent to Apple Maps);
    • Resend, to deliver emails: the confirmation email you get when you sign up, and abuse reports sent to us.
  • Authorities: the police or other authorities, when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that it is needed to protect someone's safety or to prevent or investigate fraud or crime.
  • A new owner: if Reunyt or Edge Technology Solutions is sold, merged or restructured, your information may pass to the new owner, who must protect it as this policy describes. We will tell you if this happens.
  • With your agreement: in any other case where you ask us to or agree.

7. Where it is stored

Our database and pet photos are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Some of our service providers process information in other countries, including the United States. This means your information is stored and processed outside Namibia. We choose providers that protect information to a high standard, and we only share what each needs.

When you delete your account

You can delete your account in the app's settings. It takes effect straight away.

  • Deleted at once: your account, email, profile, settings, push tokens, thank-you notes, your lost reports with their exact locations, document numbers and photos (photos within a day), the exact locations of your finds, and where your found-pet photos were taken.
  • Kept without your name for up to 90 days: found items you handed in that haven't been collected yet, so their owners can still find and collect them; your closed claims and finds; and messages you sent, which stay with the other person marked as from "Deleted account". After 90 days these are deleted too.
  • Reports of abuse: reports you made are deleted with your account. Reports other people made about you are kept, without a link to your account, until we review them and for 90 days after, so we can deal with safety issues.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed to deal with a legal claim or a safety or fraud investigation.

9. How we protect it

  • Everything sent between the app and our servers is encrypted.
  • Pet photos are stored privately. Only people who can see the post can load its photo, and nobody can change or replace it.
  • Messages can only be read by the two people in a claim, and they're deleted 90 days after the claim closes.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts stored data.
  • Rules inside our database decide who can read each piece of information, so the app can't be used to read anything you aren't allowed to see. Exact locations and document numbers can't be read by any user at all.
  • Only a small number of people at Edge Technology Solutions can access personal information, and only to run, support and protect Reunyt.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you without undue delay where the law requires it or where it could put you at risk.

10. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • change your display name and profile icon, and close or renew your reports, in the app;
  • delete your account in the app at any time;
  • turn off location, camera, microphone and notification permissions in your phone's Settings;
  • ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong, or to delete information we don't need to keep;
  • object to how we use your information, or ask us to limit it.

To make a request, email reunyt@edge.com.na from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm it's you, and we will reply within 30 days.

We respect the right to privacy in Article 13 of the Namibian Constitution. When Namibia's data protection law comes into force, we will follow it and update this policy. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right.

11. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use Reunyt. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must agree to our Terms on your behalf. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who think their child has given us information can contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
