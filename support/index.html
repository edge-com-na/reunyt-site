Using Reunyt

Where does Reunyt work?

Reunyt is coming to iPhone, starting in Namibia. You can post a report for anywhere in Namibia, and possible matches are found by distance from where the item was lost or found. The hand-in points listed in the app are in Windhoek for now.

What can I post?

Phones, wallets and purses, keys, bags, ID documents, bank cards, jewellery, electronics, clothing and anything else you've lost or found.

How long does a report stay up?

30 days, and you can renew it. A find that has been handed in stays up for 90 days after the hand-in, so its owner can still find and collect it.

What is a reference number?

Every report gets one, like RY-7K3QP. Only you see yours. Quote it at a police station, in an insurance claim, or when you contact us.

Something was stolen. Should I post it?

Yes, and report it to the police as well. Reunyt doesn't replace a police report.

Handing things over

I found an ID or a bank card. What now?

Post it, then hand it in at a police station or Home Affairs office. Driving licences and bank cards go to a police station. Write the reference number on a note with it. The owner is told where to collect it, and shows proof of identity at the counter.

I lost my ID. How will I know it was handed in?

Post a lost report and add the document number. It's never shown to anyone, and it's only used to find an exact match. When a matching find is handed in, the app tells you where. Take another document with your name, like a passport or birth certificate, and ask at the counter for found property.

How does an owner prove something is theirs?

The finder adds one to three questions only the owner could answer. You read the answers and decide. Never ask for a PIN or password: for a phone, ask the owner to unlock it in front of you.

Where should we meet?

At a police station or Home Affairs office, or a busy public place you agree on in messages. Meet in daylight where you can.

What is the handover code?

When you meet, the owner shows a code in the app, as a QR code and 4 digits. The finder scans or types it to confirm the item is back with the right person. It works without signal.

Safety and privacy

Who can see where I lost or found something?

Other people only see an area about 500 m across, never the exact place. Nobody sees your phone number or email.

How do I report a post or a person?

Tap Report on the post, conversation or claim. We review reports within 24 hours. A post reported by several people may be hidden while we check it.

How do I block someone?

Tap Block on their post, conversation or claim. They aren't told. You'll find everyone you've blocked in Settings, under Safety.

If you feel unsafe

Leave, and contact the police first. Then report the person in the app so we can act.

Notifications

How do I get alerts for my neighbourhood?

In Settings, open Notifications, then My area. Choose the area and 1, 2 or 5 km. You'll get at most 3 alerts a day. We keep only an area about 500 m across, never your exact spot.

Can I follow someone else's post?

Yes. Tap Notify me on the post, and we'll tell you when it's reunited, gets a possible match, or is closed. Who follows a post is private: its owner only sees how many people are looking out.

What do notifications show?

Only what happened, like a new match, claim or message. They never include item details, names or locations. You can turn them off in your iPhone's Settings.

Your account

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your junk or spam folder. In the app, try to sign in and tap Resend Email to get a new link. Links expire after a while and work only once.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. It's deleted straight away. Finds you handed in stay up without your name until they're collected, for up to 90 days, so their owners can still collect them.

The Privacy Policy lists what is deleted and when.

Can I get a copy of my information?

Yes. Email us from the address on your account, and we'll reply within 30 days.
