  • We never take possession of, inspect, store, transport or deliver any item.

  • We can't promise that a lost item will be found or returned.

If you are in danger, or a crime has been committed, contact the Namibian Police. If something you lost was stolen, report it to the police as well as posting it on Reunyt.
